# What I want from the Briefing

An example reader profile. Copy to interests.md and write your own; the editor reads it before every edition.

- Finance and investing first: markets, rates, deals, private equity and venture.
- Then health and biotech: trials, approvals, policy.
- Then applied AI: tools that change how work gets done, not benchmark news.
- Fewer stories, read in full, over many headlines.
